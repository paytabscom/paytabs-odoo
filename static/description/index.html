Payment Provider: PayTabs

Official PayTabs payment provider for Odoo 19. Accept cards and local payment methods across the Middle East and North Africa.

Hosted payment page

Customers are redirected to the PCI-DSS compliant PayTabs payment page and brought back to Odoo once the payment is completed. No card data ever touches your server.

The transaction is confirmed from the signed server-to-server callback PayTabs sends to your instance, so the outcome is reliable even if the customer closes the browser before returning.

  • Cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Meeza, UnionPay, OmanNet
  • Local methods: Mada, STC Pay, KNET, Samsung Pay, PayPal
  • Buy now, pay later: Tabby, Tamara, ValU, Aman, Forsa, Halan, Souhoola, Bank Installments

Refunds, manual capture and void

Refund a payment, in full or in part, straight from the Odoo transaction. Enable Capture Amount Manually to authorize the amount at checkout and capture or void it later, including partial captures and partial releases.

Declined, failed, expired and held transactions carry the reason reported by PayTabs in the chatter of the linked invoice, sales order or payment, while the customer only sees a generic message.

All PayTabs regions

United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, Kuwait, Qatar, Iraq, Morocco, Global, Madfoat and Cuzdan. Pick the region of your merchant profile; test and live profiles share the same endpoint.

Setup in minutes

  1. Install the module and open Invoicing » Configuration » Payment Providers » PayTabs.
  2. Select your Region and enter the Profile ID and Server Key from Merchant Dashboard » Developers » Key management.
  3. Set the state to Test Mode or Enabled, pick the payment methods your profile supports, and publish the provider.

The callback URL https://<your-domain>/payment/paytabs/webhook is sent with every payment request; nothing has to be configured in the PayTabs dashboard. Your Odoo instance must be reachable over HTTPS.

Built on the Odoo payment framework

Uses the standard payment module flows of Odoo 19 (redirect payment, refunds, manual capture), ships an Arabic translation and a full automated test suite, and masks the server key and signatures in the server logs.

Tokenization (saved cards) and express checkout are not part of this version.
